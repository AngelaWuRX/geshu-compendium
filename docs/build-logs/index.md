# Build Logs

The notes are what I concluded. These are how I got there, written while the
outcome was still uncertain.

The distinction matters because the two decay differently. A note gets edited
until it is right. A log is a record of a moment, including the belief that
turned out to be wrong — and that belief is usually the most useful thing on the
page, because it is the one a reader might currently hold.

!!! note "No entries yet"

    The first entries will come out of the
    [projects](../projects/index.md) as they get built.

## What goes here

- Why I replaced a component, and what actually forced it
- Rebuilding something I had already built once
- A loop, a limit or a cost that surprised me
- What broke in the first real use
- A measurement that changed my mind

## What does not

Anything that reads as a finished conclusion. That is a
[note](../notes/index.md). If the wrong turns have been edited out and the path
looks straight, it stopped being a log somewhere along the way.

An incident with real impact gets the fuller treatment in
[Postmortems](../postmortems/index.md).
